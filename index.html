● MLCI 2026  ◆  ACM · April 18–20, 2026 · Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

An Android-Based Edge Device for Footwear Defect Inspection – Toward a Portable yet Powerful Approach

Truong Gia Lam, Nguyen Minh Quang, Nguyen Thach Thanh, Huynh Viet Thang, Le Khac Kha, Phan Minh Thuan, Pham Nhi Nguyen, and Pham Cong Thien

ABSTRACT

Modern deep learning models achieve high accuracy but remain impractical for factory deployment due to computational demands requiring expensive GPU hardware. We present an end-to-end system for footwear defect inspection through mobile edge deployment. Through systematic comparison of architectures and resolutions, we identify an optimal configuration achieving 91.8% precision with real-time inference. Aggressive compression reduces size by 74% (44.8 MB to 11.5 MB) while maintaining performance, enabling deployment on commodity Android devices.

● IPMV 2026  ◆  SPIE · Proc. Vol. 14163 · Art. 14163OC

Toward an Efficient and Compact Approach for Footwear Defect Inspection Using Self-Enhancement and Data-Driven Hyperparameters

Huynh Thang, Phuc Do, Binh Nguyen, Kha Le, Ky Pham, Quan Phan, Thuan Phan, Nguyen Pham, and Thien Pham

ABSTRACT

Footwear defect inspection is critical for quality control but remains challenging due to data scarcity and class imbalance in industrial environments. This paper proposes a domain-specific approach utilizing a YOLO-based model enhanced with data-driven hyperparameter tuning and tailored augmentation. We construct a custom dataset specifically for stitching and bonding defects and demonstrate an end-to-end framework suitable for real-time edge deployment. Experimental results achieve 94.1% mAP@50 with an inference speed of 89 ms — increasing Precision by 5.91%, Recall by 7.02%, and mAP@90 by 21.55% compared to baseline configurations.
